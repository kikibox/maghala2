Benefits of side-seam drip tape

Side-seam drip tape is one of the popular options in drip irrigation systems which, due to its specific design, offers numerous advantages. This type of tape is particularly suitable for farmers with extensive irrigation needs and specific environmental conditions. The following are the key features of this irrigation tape:

Key features of side-seam drip tape

  1. Side-seam structure: Unlike other types of drip tape that have drippers, side-seam drip tape does not contain drippers; instead, it features zigzag orifices on the sides of the tape. These orifices naturally regulate water pressure and facilitate the precise emission of water droplets along specific paths. This simple and efficient design promotes greater uniformity in water distribution.
  2. Variety in flow rate and orifice size: Side-seam irrigation tape is available with various orifices, spacings, and flow rates. This feature allows farmers to customize irrigation based on the specific needs of different plants and crops. This variety in flow rates can help improve the efficiency of the irrigation system.
  3. Wide length options: Side-seam drip tape is available in various lengths, ranging from 1,000 meters to 5,000 meters and even more. This feature enables farmers to use these tapes for irrigating large and expansive farms without the need for frequent system changes.
  4. Economic and cost-effective: Economically, side-seam drip tape is much more cost-effective than plate-dripper drip tape. This feature makes it a smart choice for farmers looking to reduce irrigation costs on their farms.

Important note:

The protective clearance for side-fused tape drip irrigation lines is of great importance. To ensure long-term durability and efficiency of these lines, adherence to principles of maintenance and protection is essential. Protective measures can include preventing mechanical damage, storing them under appropriate conditions, and regularly monitoring the irrigation system's pressure status.
