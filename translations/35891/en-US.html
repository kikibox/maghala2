Key Considerations Before Purchasing a Drip Tape Irrigation Line

Before Purchase Drip Tape In drip tape irrigation, attention to several essential points can help you make the best choice for irrigation system you have. These points include:

Tape Length

  • The tape length should be proportional to your field area. Typically, drip tapes are manufactured in various lengths (1000 m, 1500 m, 3000 m). For larger fields, longer tapes are a better option.
  • When calculating tape length, the path the tape must cover and the field shape (sloped or flat) are also important factors.

Operating Pressure Resistance

  • Your water supply system must have an appropriate pressure for the drip tape to function correctly. The standard operating pressure for drip tapes is usually between 0.5 and 2 atmospheres.
  • Ensure that your selected drip tape can withstand your irrigation system pressure and site conditions (e.g., varying pressure on sloped fields).

Tape thickness

  • Drip tape thickness plays a significant role in its durability and efficiency. Thicker tapes are generally more durable but cost more. Tape thickness depends on soil type, irrigation pressure, and environmental conditions.
  • If you are in an area with heavy clay soils, it is recommended to use thicker tapes to provide greater resistance to pressure and abrasion.

Irrigation uniformity

  • One of the major advantages of using drip tapes is uniform water distribution. To ensure uniformity, the irrigation system pressure must remain constant along the line.
  • If your field is sloped or you use water sources with varying pressure, you need tapes that can maintain uniform water distribution along the entire length.

Spacing of holes or drippers

  • The appropriate spacing between holes or drippers depends on the crop type and its water requirements. For example, the recommended spacing for beans is generally about 20 cm, and for watermelon, it is 30 cm.
  • In fields with variable soil textures (e.g., sandy or clay soil), different spacings should be considered to ensure uniform irrigation.

By considering these points, you can select the appropriate drip tape for your specific site and irrigation system conditions and benefit from its optimal performance.
