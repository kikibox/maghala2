Difference Between Side-Seam and Plate-Type Drip Tape

In Comparison Drip Tape In comparing side-seam and plate-type drip tapes, various factors such as water discharge uniformity, pressure regulation, and water distribution uniformity in plates can be influential. The table below provides a detailed comparison between these two types of drip tape.

Comparison of Side-Seam Drip Tape and Plate-Type Drip Tape
Features Side-Seam Drip Tape Plate-Type Drip Tape
Structural Design Side-seam without emitters and featuring zigzag slots Emitters with uniform slots in plates
Water Discharge Uniformity High uniformity using zigzag slots Uses uniform plates for precise water discharge regulation
Pressure Regulation Via zigzag slots and side-seam Using different plates in each plate
Water Distribution Uniformity in Plates Variable based on the side-seal structure Using uniform plaques across all sections
Water pressure variation along the line Limited by zigzag seams and side seals Uniform plaques ensure equal distribution at all points
Application and Efficiency Cost-effective and suitable for larger farms Suitable for farms with specific requirements and high precision
Economic Cost Appears to be economically advantageous Generally more expensive but with higher efficiency

Note: The choice between side-seal and plaque drip tape depends on the specific needs and characteristics of each farm. Side-seal drip tape is preferred due to its Costis lower and it offers good efficiency for large farms, while plaque drip tape is suitable for farms requiring higher precision and greater control.

What should be the emission rate of the irrigation drip tape?

Required pressure for Emission rate It is suitable and uniform between 0.5 and 2 atmospheres. Below this range, discharge is low and incomplete; above this level, it can cause the drip tape to burst, creating problems for the farmer. The discharge rate of agricultural drip tape or rain irrigation tape emitters varies from 0.8 to 3.8 liters per hour, with specific rates of 1.3, 1.5, 1.6, 2, and 2.5.
