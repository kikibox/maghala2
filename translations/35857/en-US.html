Side-Seamed Drip Irrigation Tape

The side-seamed drip irrigation tape features a specific design where the water discharge holes are located on one side of the tape. This design ensures that the water flow is distributed uniformly in a single direction, preventing water wastage. This type of tape is ideal for crops that require one-sided irrigation and performs better on sloped fields or in areas where water must reach the plant roots from one side.

Features:

  • Uniform Water Distribution: Due to the specific side-seamed design, water flow is distributed uniformly on one side of the field.
  • Suitable for Sloped Fields: This type of tape is highly effective in fields with various slopes.
  • One-Sided Irrigation: The tape is designed so that water is emitted only from one side, making it ideal for crops that require irrigation from a single direction.
  • Pressure and Environmental Resistance: Thanks to its robust structure, this tape is capable of withstanding high water pressures and various environmental conditions.

Drip Irrigation Tape Emitted-Plate

Plate-equipped drip tape is a type of drip irrigation system that controls water discharge through plates installed at specific intervals. This type of tape is suitable for large agricultural fields, allowing farmers to manage water consumption precisely and efficiently due to the high accuracy of flow control. The plates are installed at varying distances, and each can regulate the water output.

Features:

  • Precise flow control: Plates enable farmers to accurately adjust water output and prevent over-consumption.
  • Variable plate spacing: Plate intervals range from 10 centimeters to one meter, which is useful for precisely meeting plant water requirements.
  • Suitable for large farms: This tape is specifically suitable for large areas that require precise and uniform irrigation.
  • Pressure resistance: Due to the robust structure of the tape and plates, this system withstands high pressure and harsh environmental conditions.
