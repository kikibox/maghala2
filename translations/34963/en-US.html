Drip line irrigation points:

Irrigation points in the system Drip line Irrigation points in the drip line system are critical, and their determination requires precision and attention. In this system, drip lines are installed in a grid pattern across the agricultural field, and the points from which water is supplied to the lines are of paramount importance.

  1. Agricultural zone analysis: First, the targeted agricultural zones must be analyzed carefully. Understanding the zones based on varying crop requirements and soil characteristics determines the required water quantity.
  2. Crop type: The type of crop planted in each zone plays a significant role in determining irrigation points. Each crop requires a specific quantity and timing of water.
  3. Soil type and land characteristics: Research on soil type and land characteristics determines the locations for irrigation. Some areas may require more or less irrigation.
  4. Land topography: The land structure and regional topography also affect the determination of irrigation points. Areas with potential water accumulation require more detailed assessment.
  5. Use of technology: The use of artificial intelligence systems and modern technologies in determining irrigation points helps improve accuracy and efficiency in irrigation.

Combining these factors helps determine suitable drip line irrigation points, enabling farmers to achieve higher water use efficiency and increased crop yields.
