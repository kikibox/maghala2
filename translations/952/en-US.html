Drip line installation steps:

Installing a drip line system in the farm requires careful attention to technical details. Below are the drip line and fitting installation steps:

  1. Field preparation:
    • First, prepare the field properly. Ensure the surface is level and free of obstacles.
  2. Mapping and design:
    • Based on the field map and plant irrigation needs, plan the drip line routing and spacing.
  3. Mainline installation:
    • Install the main irrigation line along the field and use appropriate fittings to connect the sections.
  4. Connecting the drip line to the mainline:
    • First, ensure the drip line has the appropriate fittings to connect to the mainline.
    • Use clamps or leak-proof fittings to connect the drip line to the mainline.
  5. Drip line layout in the field:
    • Drip line Install it along the field. Ensure spacing and flow rate are set correctly.
    • Cut the drip line to the required length and place it at the necessary points in the field.
  6. Drip line settings:
    • Precisely adjust the dropper spacing and water flow rate to meet plant needs.
  7. Leak check and irrigation quality verification:
    • After installation, start the system and check for leaks and irrigation quality. Repair any issues found.
  8. Ground coverage:
    • Cover the ground to protect the drip tape and prevent environmental damage.

Key points:

  • Use high-quality, leak-resistant fittings for connections.
  • Ensure the drip tape is laid correctly in the ground, preventing any deviation or bending.
  • Accurately adjust water flow rates and emitter spacing based on crop requirements.

Additionally, during installation, carefully follow the instructions and recommendations of the drip tape and fitting manufacturer.
