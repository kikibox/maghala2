What is a rain tube? 🌧️

A rain tube is a type of flexible plastic pipe used for agricultural irrigation. These pipes feature small perforations on their surface that disperse water over the ground in a rain-like pattern. This irrigation system distributes water uniformly across the field, helping to prevent water waste and improve plant growth.

Types of Rain Hoses and Their Applications for Vegetables

Below are the types of rain hoses along with their brief applications for irrigation of vegetables (such as tomato, cucumber, squash, etc.):

1. Standard Rain Hose (Standard Rain Pipe)

These pipes are typically used for large-scale fields and vegetable crops that require uniform irrigation. The small holes on these pipes facilitate even water distribution, making them ideal for plants such as tomatoes and cucumbers that require balanced moisture.

2. Misting Rain Pipe

This type of pipe is used to generate mist and high humidity in enclosed environments or greenhouses. It is suitable for vegetables such as bell peppers and cucumbers, which grow better in high-humidity conditions.

3. Mobile Rain Pipe

Mobile rain pipes are the best option for small fields and vegetable farms that require the irrigation system to be relocated. These pipes are easily moved and are suitable for crops such as tomatoes and cucumbers which require irrigation at different intervals.

4. Drip Rain Pipe

This type of pipe is used for precise and controlled irrigation. Emitters deliver water directly to the plant roots in fine, precise quantities. For vegetables that are highly sensitive to irrigation, such as eggplant or tomatoes, this type of pipe is the best option.

💧 Function of the Rain Pipe

A rain pipe is a flexible pipe made of low-density polyethylene, used to distribute water evenly in irrigation systems. The outer surface of these pipes features small, regularly arranged holes that allow water to exit as pressurized droplets or fine spray.

The operation of these pipes closely resembles natural rainfall; water is distributed evenly from above onto the field surface and penetrates the soil effectively. This irrigation method not only reduces water loss and prevents surface runoff but also improves root oxygenation and promotes balanced plant growth.

Due to their lightweight and flexible structure, rain pipes can be installed quickly, relocated easily, and used on various types of agricultural land. Consequently, they have become one of the most popular irrigation methods in medium and large-scale farms.

⚙️ Technical Specifications of Rain Pipes

  • Material: Low-density polyethylene with high resistance to sunlight and chemicals
  • Common diameter: There are various models, but 63 mm rain pipe One of the most commonly used options
  • Roll length: Usually available in lengths from 100 meters to 400 meters
  • Suitable working pressure: Approximately 1 to 4 bar, depending on crop type and soil conditions
  • Spray type: Adjustable coarse or fine spray (mist) depending on the orifices
