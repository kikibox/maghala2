Measuring water pressure on drip tape

Measuring water pressure for drip tape is one of the most important factors in the correct implementation of an irrigation system. Water pressure is the force that water exerts on the drip tape and is used as a primary parameter for adjusting and optimizing the efficiency of a drip irrigation system.

To measure water pressure in the drip line, you can use dedicated water pressure measuring tools. These tools are generally known as “manometers” . Manometers are manufactured as devices featuring various voltage display gauges.

When measuring water pressure, note the following steps:

  1. Connecting the manometer: Connect the manometer near the drip line connection. This can be done by attaching it to a water valve or the irrigation system.
  2. Pressure reading: After connecting the manometer, introduce water into the irrigation system. The manometer display will indicate the water pressure in the unit used (typically bar or pounds per square inch).
  3. Pressure adjustment: Adjust the water pressure based on crop requirements and the irrigation system. Each drip line requires a specific operating pressure range, which must be adhered to according to the type and specifications of the drip line.
  4. Inspection and adjustment: Check the water pressure periodically and make adjustments as needed. Seasonal changes, pipe blockages, or other factors may require recalibration.
  5. Pressure logging: Record the water pressure regularly to ensure it remains within the specified range for the drip line.

Note that determining water pressure must be done accurately to ensure optimal drip line performance and water conservation.
