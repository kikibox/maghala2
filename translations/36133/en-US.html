What is the yield of white onions per hectare?

White onion is one of the most popular onion varieties in domestic and export markets. This onion cultivar is widely used in the food industry, packaging, and fresh consumption due to its sharper taste, thin skin, and glossy white color. However, the primary question for farmers is: What is the yield of white onions per hectare?

Management Type White Onion Yield per Hectare
Traditional 20 to 30 tons
Semi-mechanized 30 to 45 tons
Mechanized with drip irrigation and precise management 50 to 65 tons or more

Factors affecting increased white onion yield

To achieve maximum production, several key factors must be managed correctly:

  • Selection of appropriate variety and seed: Use of improved seed with high germination rates and suitable for the local climatic conditions.
  • Correct planting method and spacing:
    • Spacing between plants: 10 to 15 centimeters
    • Spacing between rows: 30 to 45 centimeters
  • Drip irrigation: Using the help of tape or threaded polyethylene tape, uniform irrigation and water conservation are possible. This method promotes uniform growth and increases onion weight.
  • Targeted fertilization: Nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, and micronutrient fertilizers should be adjusted based on soil test results and applied via fertigation (injection into irrigation water).
  • Disease and pest control: Diseases such as downy mildew, purple spot, and rot can be managed with preventive measures to prevent yield loss.
