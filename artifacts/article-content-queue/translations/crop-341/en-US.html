Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How does the cost of tape drip compare to drip irrigation?

Tape drip is generally less expensive than drip irrigation systems because of its simpler structure. However, its durability may be lower. For green onion cultivation, which requires rapid and uniform water delivery, tape drip is an economical and efficient option.

2. Can costs be reduced by soil grading?

Yes. Proper soil grading prevents water accumulation in specific areas and ensures more uniform distribution. This reduces excess water usage and associated pumping costs.

3. What is the optimal time for scallion cultivation with drip tape?

The optimal timing varies by regional climate, but autumn or spring planting (for an autumn harvest) is common. In any case, complete field preparation and irrigation installation must be finished before planting.

4. How can I reduce the electricity costs of irrigation pumps?

Using high-efficiency pumps, adjusting system pressure appropriately, and utilizing solar energy (if possible) can significantly reduce energy costs. Additionally, irrigating during cooler hours (early morning or night) prevents water evaporation and conserves useful energy.
