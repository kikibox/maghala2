Why is the drip line system a necessity in green onion cultivation?

Green onion (Allium cepa var. aggregatum) is a valuable and high-demand crop that exhibits rapid growth and high sensitivity to rhizosphere conditions. Under traditional methods, flood irrigation can lead to root fungal diseases and lateral stem decay, which directly impact the green onion yield per hectare using drip line systems negatively. The drip line system, by precisely distributing water and nutrients at the soil surface, creates ideal conditions for the formation of uniform, small green onions.

The use of this irrigation method not only reduces water consumption by up to 40 percent but also decreases plant stress by maintaining uniform moisture in the root zone, thereby increasing the economic attractiveness of investment in the agricultural sector. Below, we examine the technical and practical principles of this method.

Key parameters in determining green onion yield

The effect of planting density on production volume

One of the main factors farmers must consider is appropriate planting density. For green onions, the final spacing between plants should generally allow sufficient room for root system development without competition for light and nutrients. Higher densities are acceptable in light soil textures, while in light and heavy soils, reducing spacing benefits unit yield production.

Integrated fertilization and irrigation management (Fertigation)

Drip tape allows you to apply pesticides and mineral fertilizers directly with irrigation water. This method prevents nutrient leaching and optimizes the uptake of micronutrients such as manganese, zinc, and iron, which are essential for onion seedling growth. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is tape drip suitable for all soil types?

The tape drip system is generally designed for most agricultural soils, but in soils with high salinity or extreme acidity, chemical adjustments are required. In specific areas, using high-quality [agricultural tape drip connectors](https://navar-abyari.ir/%d8%a7%d8%aa%d8%b5%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%86%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b1-%d8%aa%db%8c%d9%84-%da%a9%d8%b4%d8%a7%d9%88%d8%b1%d8%b2%db%8c/) increases the system's lifespan.

What is the difference between tape drip and standard drip for green onion seedlings?

Tape drip creates a moisture band, enabling simultaneous irrigation and planting, which is more efficient for larger green onion farms with compact, dense spacing. Standard drip is suitable for plants planted at wider intervals.
