Factors determining onion seed rate per hectare

Before any calculation, you must understand that the final onion yield is directly related to planting density and seed quality. Old or weak seeds always require higher consumption. Below are the most important influencing factors:

  • Seed quality and vigor: Seeds with vigor above 95% require a lower quantity compared to weak seeds. Always review the seed lab report before purchasing.
  • Onion variety type: Local varieties exhibit different seed consumption rates and ground coverage behaviors compared to improved varieties.
  • Cultivation objective: If the objective is harvesting green shoots, the seeding density differs from that required when the goal is producing large bulbs.

It is generally recommended to use between 15 and 25 kilograms of seed per hectare for conventional onion seedling cultivation, although this figure may vary depending on environmental conditions. Impact of drip and tape irrigation systems on yield

One of the major challenges in onion seedling cultivation is managing water delivery to its small and sensitive roots. In dry conditions, excessive spraying can cause surface soil erosion and stress the plant. In contrast, drip and tape irrigation systems allow for precise water delivery to the root zone. When you onion seed rate per hectare adjust based on your designed irrigation system, you can maximize yield.

Precise calculation and practical sowing tips

For quality Onion Set sowing, adherence to standard cultivation protocols is essential. Sowing too deeply can delay germination, as Onion Set is a shallow-rooted plant. Below are the practical steps for sowing and seed management:

  1. Seedbed preparation: Soil must be prepared for vegetable cultivation by addressing chemical pesticide concentrations, salinity, and texture.
  2. Sowing Onion Set: Sow the seeds at a depth of 1 to 2 cm and cover them with appropriate soil.
  3. Start of irrigation: The first irrigation after sowing must be applied with low pressure and care to prevent seed washing.
  4. Growth monitoring: If seedling density is lower than expected, gap-fill seeds into bare spots.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is the best time for planting green onions per hectare?

The best planting time depends on the geographic region and climate. In cold regions, autumn planting usually requires less seed because the plants have more time to grow and establish. In warm regions, spring and summer planting (using shade netting) are more common.

2. Can drip tape be used for green onions?

Yes, drip tape is one of the best options for green onions because it increases moisture around the plants and reduces surface evaporation. However, ensure that the output of the tape does not cause waterlogging around the stem, which can lead to fungal diseases. To correctly adjust water pressure in these systems, it is recommended to study [Drip Tape Water Pressure Measurement Guide](https://navar-abyari.ir/measuring-drip-tape-water-pressure-iq/).

3. What should you do if green onion seed quality is poor?

If you do not have seed backing, always increase the seed application rate by 20 to 30 percent. You can also complete ground coverage by making repeated plantings in empty areas. Precise seed management means always working with high-quality seed at the right time.

4. What is the appropriate planting distance for green onions?

Row spacing is typically 20 to 30 cm, and in-row spacing is 5 to 10 cm. These dimensions help you calculate the required seed quantity more accurately.
