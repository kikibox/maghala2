Mung bean seed rate per hectare based on sowing methods

The mung bean sowing method is one of the most important factors determining the required seed quantity. Common mung bean sowing methods include broadcast, row, and mixed sowing. Each of these methods affects the mung bean seed count per hectare. In broadcast sowing, seeds are distributed uniformly across one hectare, and the seed count directly impacts the number of plants per hectare and the space for root growth. In row sowing, seeds are planted in designated rows, and the seed count directly impacts the number of plants per hectare and the space for root growth. In mixed sowing, a combination of broadcast and row methods is used, and the seed count directly impacts the number of plants per hectare and the space for root growth. Under drip and strip irrigation conditions, row sowing is recommended to ensure mung bean seeds are planted uniformly per hectare, allowing plants to grow well and access their root zones.
